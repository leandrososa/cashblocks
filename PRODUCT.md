# Cashblocks Customer Terminal

## Users

Bank customers use the terminal while standing in a public, time-sensitive
environment with variable lighting, familiarity, language, vision, mobility,
and cognitive needs. Service operators use a separate, deliberately
de-emphasized maintenance path.

## Product Purpose

Cashblocks provides a trustworthy self-service banking experience that moves a
customer from physical or cardless identification to a completed transaction
with minimal ambiguity. A production terminal is activated by hardware events,
not simulated on-screen hardware controls. While idle, the screen can carry
bank campaigns without delaying or competing with the next customer action.

## Brand Personality

Classic, clear, and trustworthy. The experience should feel established and
calm rather than fashionable, playful, luxurious, or technically experimental.

## Anti-references

- Dark navy terminal dashboards and developer-console aesthetics.
- Neon fintech, crypto, gaming, or glassmorphism visual language.
- Simulated hardware buttons, decorative status breadcrumbs, or controls that
  do not correspond to a customer action.
- Advertising that resembles a transaction control, obscures accessibility
  guidance, or delays activation after a card or touch event.
- Publicly prominent operator controls.

## Design Principles

1. Hardware leads, the screen responds.
2. One customer decision should dominate each transactional screen.
3. Advertising yields immediately and completely to banking activity.
4. Familiar banking conventions should reduce hesitation and error.
5. Operational controls stay available without competing with customer tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with high text and control contrast, large touch targets,
visible focus, keyboard and assistive-technology support, non-color status
cues, and a reduced-motion mode. The interface should support Spanish and
English without relying on text embedded in campaign images.
